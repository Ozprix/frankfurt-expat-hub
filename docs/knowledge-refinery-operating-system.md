# Frankfurt Answer Library: Operating System

## Purpose

Turn fragmented Frankfurt relocation information into trustworthy, plain-English answers. The product is not legal, tax, immigration, insurance, or medical advice. It helps readers prepare, verify, and know when to use a qualified professional.

## Publication rule

No answer page publishes until every material claim has a primary source, a last-verified date, a next-review date, and a named reviewer. A page with a stale critical record must show an update warning or be withdrawn.

## Source order

1. Government office or statutory source.
2. Official form, PDF, or service portal.
3. Written confirmation from a relevant provider, labelled as provider-confirmed.
4. Community experience, labelled as a dated practical tip and never used as the official rule.

Do not copy official text. Summarise it in plain English and link to the original source.

## Status rules

| Status | Meaning | Public use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planned | Important topic, not researched | Never public |
| Researching | Sources collected, claims not checked | Never public |
| Reviewed | Sources checked, reviewer and dates recorded | May be public |
| Stale | A required review is overdue or source changed | Warning or unpublish |
| Retired | No longer reliable or useful | Remove from public library |

## Review cadence

- Weekly: appointment availability, office hours, current process observations, volatile links.
- Monthly: documents, forms, official requirements, and high-stakes immigration/insurance/tax guidance.
- Quarterly: evergreen explainers and low-stakes setup information.
- Immediately: official change, credible reader correction, or material provider report.

## Answer-page standard

Every published page needs:

1. A direct answer in the opening 100 words.
2. Scope: who the answer applies to and what it does not cover.
3. Frankfurt-specific steps and bilingual document names where relevant.
4. A visible official-source section.
5. Last verified date, scheduled review date, and a correction link.
6. One primary next action only: checklist, tool, directory, or referral request.
7. A concise non-advice boundary for high-risk topics.

## Trust and commercial separation

- Paid partners never affect factual claims or organic ordering.
- Paid/referral relationships are visibly labelled.
- Provider listings are not endorsements or legal/medical/tax advice.
- Corrections are logged with the date and material change.
