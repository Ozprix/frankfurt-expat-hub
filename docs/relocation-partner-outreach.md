# Relocation-Help Partner Outreach

Goal: sign 1–2 paying referral partners before driving traffic to `/relocation-help`.
Source: existing scraped directory (`src/data/scraped/directory.json`).

## Who to skip (won't do per-referral with a solo)
Self-serve products and global firms — they don't pay per closed referral:
- Tax SaaS: SteuerGo, Wundertax, Taxfix
- Insurance aggregators / products: Feather, Ottonova
- Booking platforms: Doctolib
- Enterprise immigration: Fragomen (global firm, won't deal per-lead)

Aim at **boutique / owner-run firms** — they need clients and can say yes fast.

## Deal to propose
- **Free to start**, referral fee only on **closed** clients (10–15% of their fee, or flat €50–150/referral).
- Move to per-lead pricing later, once conversion is proven.
