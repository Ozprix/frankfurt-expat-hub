Anmeldung in Frankfurt

After you actually move into a Frankfurt home, register your residence within two weeks. Use the online route only if you meet its identity requirements; otherwise use any Frankfurt Citizens’ Office.

Scope: This is general registration guidance. It does not decide your immigration status or residence-permit requirements.

Last verified: 2026-08-11   Next review: 2026-09-11

What to do

  1. Check whether the online main/sole-residence service fits your case.
  2. Otherwise attend a Frankfurt Bürgeramt, or use a trusted representative with written authority where accepted.
  3. Bring the original identity documents and original Wohnungsgeberbestätigung for each person registering.

Documents and terms

  • Anmeldeformular / registration form
  • Wohnungsgeberbestätigung / confirmation from the landlord
  • Original passport or ID; non-EU residents should also bring an electronic residence permit if available
