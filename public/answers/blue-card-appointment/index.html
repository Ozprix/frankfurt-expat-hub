Blue Card appointment preparation

For Frankfurt’s EU Blue Card process, register your Frankfurt residence first and submit the application while your visa is valid (or within 90 days where you are visa-exempt). The City requires all requested scans before the online application can be sent.

Scope: Requirements depend on your profession and case. This is not legal advice and it does not promise an appointment or processing time.

Last verified: 2026-08-11   Next review: 2026-08-18

What to do

  1. Check that your job offer, qualification, and salary meet the City’s current requirements.
  2. Prepare clear scans of every document listed for your case before starting the online application.
  3. Submit within the relevant status deadline and respond to any request for additional documents.

Documents and terms

  • Gültiger Nationalpass / valid national passport
  • Arbeitsvertrag oder verbindliches Arbeitsplatzangebot / employment contract or binding job offer
  • Arbeitgeberbescheinigung / employer certificate
  • Akademischer Abschluss plus Anabin-Nachweis / degree plus Anabin equivalence proof for a foreign degree
  • Mietvertrag and current-rent bank statement / lease and current-rent bank statement
