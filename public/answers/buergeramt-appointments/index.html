Frankfurt Bürgeramt appointments

Frankfurt generally requires appointments on Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday. Main Citizens’ Offices offer walk-in consultation on Monday and Wednesday, but waits can be long and ticket issue can stop early.

Scope: Hours, appointment releases, and availability change. Use the live City page before travelling; this page does not promise a waiting time or appointment.

Last verified: 2026-08-11   Next review: 2026-08-18

What to do

  1. Check the City’s appointment service and live wait information.
  2. Use any eligible Frankfurt Citizens’ Office, not only the office nearest your address.
  3. Bring an interpreter if you need one; the City advises this for people who do not speak enough German.

Documents and terms

  • Terminbestätigung / appointment confirmation, if booked
  • Unterlagen für Ihr Anliegen / documents for your request
