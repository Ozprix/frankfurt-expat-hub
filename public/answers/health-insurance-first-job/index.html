Health insurance for a first job

For 2026, employees earning more than €603 per month and at or below €77,400 per year are generally compulsorily insured through statutory health insurance (GKV). Above that annual threshold, the choice and consequences need individual care.

Scope: This is general information, not personal financial, insurance, or medical advice. Thresholds change and insurance choices can have long-term consequences.

Last verified: 2026-08-11   Next review: 2026-09-11

What to do

  1. Confirm your expected annual salary and employment status with your employer.
  2. If you are in statutory insurance, choose an insurer and obtain a Mitgliedsbescheinigung when needed.
  3. If considering private insurance, compare coverage and long-term implications with an independent qualified adviser or insurer before deciding.

Documents and terms

  • Mitgliedsbescheinigung / membership certificate
  • Gesetzliche Krankenversicherung (GKV) / statutory health insurance
  • Private Krankenversicherung (PKV) / private health insurance
  • Jahresarbeitsentgeltgrenze (JAEG) / annual earnings threshold
