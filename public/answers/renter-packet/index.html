Frankfurt renter packet checklist

Frankfurt has no official universal renter-packet or SCHUFA substitute. Prepare only the documents a provider asks for, minimise sensitive information, and ask in advance what alternative evidence they will consider if you have no German credit history.

Scope: Private landlords and agents decide what evidence they accept. This page does not guarantee an application outcome or advise you to send unnecessary personal data.

Last verified: 2026-08-11   Next review: 2026-11-11

What to do

  1. Ask the provider which documents it will accept before uploading sensitive material.
  2. Prepare requested ID, income or employment evidence, and only the exact credit/reference evidence requested.
  3. If you are new to Germany, ask what alternative evidence the provider will consider; acceptance is its decision.

Documents and terms

  • Mietvertrag / rental agreement, if you already have one
  • Arbeitsvertrag / employment contract
  • Gehaltsabrechnungen / payslips
  • SCHUFA-Bonitätsauskunft / SCHUFA credit-status report, only if requested
