Tax ID not received: follow-up steps

Your tax ID (Steueridentifikationsnummer or IdNr) is assigned automatically on first registration in Germany and stays the same for life. If it has not arrived within three months, request a renewed notification through the Federal Central Tax Office.

Scope: This is a Germany-wide federal process, not a tax-advice page. Do not use third-party tax-ID request sites.

Last verified: 2026-08-11   Next review: 2026-09-11

What to do

  1. Check a previous tax assessment or wage-tax certificate first.
  2. After three months from first German registration, use the BZSt’s official renewed-notification process.
  3. Do not expect the BZSt to disclose the number by phone or email.

Documents and terms

  • Steueridentifikationsnummer (IdNr) / tax identification number
  • Lohnsteuerbescheinigung / wage-tax certificate
  • Einkommensteuerbescheid / income-tax assessment
