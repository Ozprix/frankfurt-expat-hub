Temporary housing and Anmeldung

Temporary housing is not automatically exempt from registration. The answer depends on where you are already registered, how long you will stay, and whether the provider can issue a valid Wohnungsgeberbestätigung after you move in.

Scope: The legal timing rules have exceptions. Ask the provider and Frankfurt’s Bürgeramt or 115 about your exact arrangement before relying on a temporary address.

Last verified: 2026-08-11   Next review: 2026-09-11

What to do

  1. Confirm whether you are already registered at another German address.
  2. Confirm the actual intended length of stay.
  3. Ask the accommodation provider in writing whether it can issue a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung after move-in.

Documents and terms

  • Wohnungsgeberbestätigung / confirmation from the landlord, if registration is required
  • Passport or ID / Reisepass oder Ausweis
