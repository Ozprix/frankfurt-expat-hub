Wohnungsgeberbestätigung explained

For Anmeldung, a rental agreement alone is not enough. You need a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung confirming the move-in; for a sublet, the main tenant can be the Wohnungsgeber.

Scope: This page explains the registration confirmation, not whether a landlord must rent to you or the terms of your tenancy.

Last verified: 2026-08-11   Next review: 2026-09-11

What to do

  1. Request the confirmation after your actual move-in.
  2. Check that the names, address, move-in date, and issuer details are correct.
  3. Bring the original confirmation to registration, or use it in the eligible online process.

Documents and terms

  • Wohnungsgeberbestätigung / confirmation from the landlord
  • Mietvertrag / rental agreement (helpful context, but not a replacement for the confirmation)
